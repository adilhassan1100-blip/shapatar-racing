SHAPATAR RACING

Karachi freestyle bike race on Shahrah-e-Faisal: FTC se Baloch Colony tak. Chai piyo, nitro maaro, laat maaro, jeeto!

▶ KHELO (PLAY)

Free, no download. First load takes a minute; after that it's fast.

📱 Phone par

  • Phone ko landscape (aara) karo
  • Left thumb: < > steering, < LAAT
  • Right thumb: NITRO, BRAKE, LAAT >
  • Seedha chalate waqt bike khud lait jati hai (Superman!)
  • Best in Chrome. Purane phones par thora slow ho sakta hai.

💻 PC / Laptop par

  • A D steering
  • W lait jao (tez)
  • Space nitro, S brake
  • Q / E left / right laat
  • 1 2 3 Asaan / Theek thaak / Mushkil

🏆 Kaise jeeto

  • Chai ke cup uthao: full nitro
  • Gaariyon ke paas se nikal ke NEAR MISS: nitro bharta hai
  • Takkar se bacho, rivals ko laat maaro
  • Aakhri 500m mein koi madad nahi. Jo acha chalaye, woh jeete!
