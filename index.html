SHAPATAR RACING

Karachi freestyle bike race on Shahrah-e-Faisal: FTC se Baloch Colony tak. Chai piyo, nitro maaro, laat maaro, jeeto!

▶ KHELO (PLAY)

Free, no download. First load takes a minute; after that it's fast.

🤖 Android app download (APK, 57 MB)

Sirf Android. Download ke baad file kholo; phone poochay to "Install unknown apps" allow karo, phir Install.

📱 Phone par

  • Phone ko landscape (aara) karo
  • Left thumb: < > steering, < LAAT
  • Right thumb: NITRO, BRAKE, LAAT >
  • Seedha chalate waqt bike khud lait jati hai (Superman!)
  • Best in Chrome. Purane phones par thora slow ho sakta hai.

💻 PC / Laptop par

  • A D steering
  • W lait jao (tez)
  • Space nitro, S brake
  • Q / E left / right laat
  • 1 2 3 4 Asaan / Theek thaak / Mushkil / Endless

🏆 Kaise jeeto

  • Poyon Oil uthao: full nitro
  • Gaariyon ke paas se nikal ke NEAR MISS: nitro bharta hai
  • Takkar se bacho, rivals ko laat maaro
  • Aakhri 500m mein koi madad nahi. Jo acha chalaye, woh jeete!

🌐 Online - dosto ke saath

  • ONLINE KHELO dabao, ek dost HOST kare
  • Baaki 4-letter code daal ke JOIN karein (8 tak)
  • RACE ya LAST MAN STANDING: jis ki takkar na ho, woh jeete
  • Pehli dafa server jagne mein ek minute lag sakta hai

♾️ Endless

  • Koi rival nahi, speed aur traffic barhta jata hai
  • Ek takkar aur khatam. Apna best score toro!
